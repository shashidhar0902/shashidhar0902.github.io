ARRIVAL RATE (λ)

Poisson-distributed rate of customers entering. Uniform distribution → 9–10 students arriving per minute.

λNV=6.67   λVEG=1.67

SERVICE RATE (μ)

Exponentially distributed. 9 items × ~7 sec = 60 sec per student. Each server handles 1 student/minute.

μ = 1 student/min/server

M/M/c STABILITY

System is stable only when utilisation ρ < 1. If ρ ≥ 1, queue grows without bound.

ρ = λ/(c·μ) < 1 required

LITTLE'S LAW

L = λ × W. Average students in system = arrival rate × average time each student spends.

W = Wq + 1/μ

Recommendations

❌ NOT FEASIBLE
📅
Option A: Fixed Batch Arrivals
Stagger 1,000 students into time-slot batches to reduce simultaneous λ.
  • 4 batches of 250 students at staggered intervals
  • Reduces λ to ~2–3/min → ρ < 1 theoretically
  • Students have varied class & event schedules
  • Feast is social — staggered entry kills shared energy
  • High non-compliance risk, impractical to enforce
  • Operationally complex to monitor & implement
∴ Valid in theory — impractical for real-life feast context.
✅ RECOMMENDED
➕
Option B: Add More Servers
Scale server count to bring ρ below 1 for both sub-queues on feast day.
  • Add 5 NV servers → total 7 NV servers
  • Add 1 VEG server → total 2 VEG servers
  • NV ρ = 6.67 ÷ 7 = 0.95 ✓ STABLE
  • VEG ρ = 1.67 ÷ 2 = 0.84 ✓ STABLE
  • No change to student behaviour required
  • Hire casual staff only on feast days — cost-efficient
∴ Feasible, effective & operationally simple. Preferred solution.
